## 1. Executive Summary

This master redesign successfully transforms the Satanipara Community prototype into a world-class digital platform that synthesizes:
- **International Editorial Polish:** Asymmetric compositions, rich figure captions, documentary photography, and milestone ledgers.
- **Apple-Inspired Product Refinement:** Precise micro-interactions, responsive 1-column to 4-column flow, dynamic header-anchored mobile drawers with background scroll locking, and zero-collision floating HUD.
- **Bengali First Identity:** Dignified Tiro Bangla display headings, legible Anek Bangla body prose, and tested complex Bengali conjuncts (যুক্তাক্ষর).

All 35 screens and 8 interactive user flows have been preserved, audited, verified across 8 viewports, and documented in comprehensive technical reports.
